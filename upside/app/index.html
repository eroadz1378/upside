How picks work: every horizon scores the universe on news sentiment, analyst revisions, price momentum, technical setup, unusual volume, rising Reddit attention, recent earnings surprises and what members of Congress have disclosed buying or selling, then ranks stocks by overall score and shows the top one with its model upside. Momentum favors stocks with a strong 6-month trend that have pulled back recently. Targets are adjusted for the overall market's mood and for how closely each stock moves with the S&P 500. Forecast bands are model ranges, not guarantees. Markets can and do move against any forecast.
